version 1.0 - TBD
NEW:
  Low Voltage Wire item.
  Battery item.
  Added Solar Panels:
   - By default each solar panel provides 0.5 energy per tick.
   - Solar panels can only be placed on top of blocks.
   - Since solar panels sit flat at all times, energy calculation changes during the day, with 100%
       energy when sun is at noon, and 0% when sun is at horizon. Energy calculation follows a sine curve.
   - Solar Panels use a hitscan to determine if line-of-sight to the sun is blocked.
      - This can be changed in the config if it's using too much processing power.
   - Solar Panels generate less energy if a thunderstorm is occuring.
   - Solar Panels (by default) get dirty over time, which lowers the power generated. Clean solar panels
       by washing with water, or waiting until it rains.
   - Solar Panels do not connect to Energy Wire directly, there must be a Solar Panel Controller adjacent
       to a Solar Panel.
   - Right-clicking the Solar Panel Controller will give you detailed info about the solar array.
  Charger machine, which can charge batteries and items (and probably items from other mods.)
  Energy tools, which need to be charged to function.
   - Same mining level and number of uses as Diamond tools.
   - Same speed and attack strength as Iron tools.
CHANGED:
  Another Energy Rewrite:
   - Energy networks will now prioritize 'free' energy sources, such as Solar Panels.
   - The Generator will now 'use' energy every tick since it's supposed to be burning fuel.
   - Batteries will no longer balance their energy by default, but this can be changed in the config.
   - The Universal Energy Interface is now much more customizable.
     - Reintroduced the 'Internal' and 'External' transfer modes that were lost since version 1.3.1.
  The Generator now has a constant extract rate of 5 energy per tick regardless of the item being consumed.
FIXED:
  Machines will now immediately update their data if it was changed in the config. Previously they
    would only update if you removed the machine and placed it back down again.
  Fix Passive Machines (Plasma Generator and Crystal Matter Generator) continuing to accept energy even though
    the output was full.
  Passive Machines can now have their output items be extracted using a Hopper.
  The Electric Furance now adjusts work time based on the cooking time of the item being smelted.



For older version history, please see Overpowered Technology changelog before version 6.
